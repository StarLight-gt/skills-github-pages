Why invest in the first place?

Investing helps your money grow over time so you can keep up with rising costs, build long-term security, and reach the goals that matter to you. Instead of letting cash sit idle, a planned investment strategy puts your money to work so you can target higher returns over the long run.

Whether you are in Malaysia or Singapore, inflation and rising living costs can quietly reduce purchasing power. Investing early gives you more time for your money to compound and work through market cycles.

What is a Unit Trust?

A Unit Trust pools money from many investors and is managed by professional fund managers. The fund can invest across asset classes such as equities, bonds, or mixed portfolios depending on the investment objective.

You buy units in the fund, and the value moves with the performance of the underlying investments. This makes Unit Trusts a convenient way to access diversified portfolios.

  • Professional management and research.
  • Diversification to spread risk.
  • Options for different risk profiles.
  • Regular savings plans for disciplined investing.

Investing through life stages

Student

Focus on building the habit. Start with small, regular contributions and prioritize balanced or growth-oriented funds to give your money time to compound.

Working adult

Increase contributions as income grows. Combine growth funds with some defensive options to manage volatility while still aiming for long-term returns.

Starting a family

Align investments with milestones like housing, education, and protection needs. Consider a diversified mix and set aside a portion for medium-term goals.

Retirement

Shift towards capital preservation and income-oriented funds. The goal is to protect accumulated savings while maintaining a stable cash flow.
